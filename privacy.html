SamDLab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

SamDLab is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the SamDLab Etsy shop (hahadoo) and its Pinterest account.

What we access

With my permission, SamDLab connects to my own Pinterest and Etsy accounts to read my boards, pins and listings, and to create and schedule pins of my own products.

What we store

Access tokens are stored securely on my own computer and are never shared. Pin details (title, description, link, board, posting date) are saved locally so I can track what was posted.

What we don't do

SamDLab does not collect data about other Pinterest users, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.

Removing access

I can disconnect SamDLab at any time from Pinterest or Etsy account settings, and delete the locally stored data.
